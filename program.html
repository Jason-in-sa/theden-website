OUR TRAINING PROGRAMS

From daily functional workouts to official HYROX race preparation.

Track Breakdown

1. HYROX Competition Track

Specific station training including sled push/pull, SkiErg, rowing, burpee broad jumps, farmers carry, and wall balls combined with targeted running intervals.

2. Daily Functional Conditioning

High-energy group workouts designed to build cardiovascular capacity, core power, and functional strength for daily life performance.

3. High School Athlete Development

Off-season and in-season athletic development programs tailored for school sports teams (rugby, netball, athletics, swimming).
