ABOUT THE DEN

Empowering high school athletes and the local community through world-class functional fitness.

Our Mission

To build stronger humans through professional coaching, structured conditioning, and an inclusive training community.

The Sandringham High School Rejuvenation

Once a shuttered gym space, The Den was restored through alumni support to become a vital hub for Sandringham High School. Today, it serves as an epicenter for sports development, offering specialized training for students, alumni, and local residents.

Expert Coaching Staff

Our team consists of certified HYROX specialists and strength coaches dedicated to movement quality, injury prevention, and athletic growth.
