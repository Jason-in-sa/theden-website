ABOUT THE DEN

Our history, mission, and expert coaching staff at Sandringham High School.

Our Mission

To provide high-performance functional training accessible to both high school athletes and the local community, fostering discipline, strength, and lifelong physical health.

Our Origin Story

Founded as a partnership initiative to upgrade school athletic infrastructure, The Den was developed into a full-scale HYROX affiliated functional training center serving local residents and high school sports teams alike.
